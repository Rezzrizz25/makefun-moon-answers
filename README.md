After four billion years of silent listening, the Moon finally replies — one line, in its own voice, to everyone who ever wished, howled, or made a promise at 2 a.m.

> Okay, fine — after four billion years of wishes, howls and 2 a.m. promises, I'm answering: no, I can't make him text you back.

## Run it

```bash
node main.ts
```

This writes the entry line to `entry.txt` inside `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR` (default `/output`).
